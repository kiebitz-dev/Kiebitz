Stockfish 19
============

Kiebitz includes Stockfish, a free UCI chess engine developed by the Stockfish
authors and contributors. Stockfish is distributed under the GNU General Public
License version 3 (GPL-3.0). The complete license is included next to this file
as COPYING.txt.

Kiebitz starts Stockfish as a separate process and communicates with it through
the public UCI text protocol. Stockfish is not modified by Kiebitz and no part
of Stockfish is linked into the Kiebitz binary.

--------------------------

Binary provenance
-----------------

The Android arm64 engine is compiled without source modifications from the
exact commit above. Kiebitz uses Android NDK 28.2.13676358, the generic armv8
target, and 16-KB ELF LOAD alignment. The reproducible build commands and
the checksum verification of the NNUE networks are in
scripts/build-stockfish-android.ps1 and .github/workflows/release.yml.

The Windows x86-64 engine is taken from the unmodified official release
archive named in the link below. Kiebitz verifies this SHA-256 hash before
packaging and aborts on a mismatch:

https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/releases/download/sf_19/stockfish-windows-x86-64-universal.zip
SHA-256: 3c8bf1f9ea66a09350a40df4f632288285ac206d99f33ab5842c408fc30b48a7
